Insurance & Receipts

Coverage, payment and cancellation details at a glance

Extended health plans

Official receipts are issued after each paid session for submission to your insurer. Many extended health plans cover psychotherapy with a Registered Psychotherapist (Q) — please confirm your specific coverage with your provider before starting.

Payment

Sessions are paid by e-transfer or credit card at the time of booking. Payment details are confirmed when your appointment is scheduled.

Cancellations

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to change or cancel an appointment. Late cancellations may be charged the full session fee.

A note on access

If you do not have insurance or finances are a barrier, please connect with me to discuss low cost therapy. I'm happy to talk through what might be workable.
