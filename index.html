Meet Beenish Jalil

Registered Psychotherapist (Q)

I am Beenish, a registered psychotherapist (Q) dedicated to providing a safe and supportive space where you can feel heard and understood. I work with children, adults and families experiencing stress, anxiety, trauma, relationship challenges, grief and self esteem issues. I provide Trauma informed care and work with modalities such as CBT, DBT, mindfulness, somatic approach, psychodynamic psychotherapy and solution focused therapy.

I believe that every person has the capacity for growth and healing, and I strive to help my clients work through their challenges, heal and grow. I offer therapy in English, Urdu and Hindi. I look forward to supporting you on your journey towards emotional well being.
