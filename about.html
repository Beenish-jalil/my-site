Mental Health and Wellness Matters

Whether you are seeking one-on-one psychotherapy or an organization looking for relevant mental health and wellness support by way of training and facilitation, coaching and program development, Heart To Soul has what you are looking for. Mental health is a critical component for overall wellbeing and I am committed to helping my clients achieve their goals in a supportive and empowering environment.

My Story

My journey to becoming a psychotherapist has been shaped by both my professional experiences and my personal life. As a mother of three wonderful children, I understand the joys, challenges, and constant balancing act that life can bring. Before becoming a psychotherapist, I practiced in the medical field as a doctor, where I learned the importance of compassion, trust, and caring for people during vulnerable moments. I have always been drawn to helping people. As a medical practitioner, I found myself listening to my patients' stories and realized that sometimes, being truly heard can be just as healing as any treatment. I believe that feeling heard, understood, and accepted has a profound impact on a person's well-being. This passion for supporting others and understanding their emotional experiences inspired me to pursue psychotherapy. Today, I have the privilege of supporting children, teens, adults, and families as they navigate life's challenges. My goal is to provide a safe, compassionate, and non-judgmental space where you can explore your emotions, build resilience, and discover your strengths. It is an honor to walk alongside my clients on their journey toward healing, growth, and a more fulfilling life.

My Approach and Principles

I am guided by core principles that shape every interaction and service I provide

Safe Space

I create a safe, inclusive space for people to connect, to be heard and feel valued. Your wellbeing and comfort are my top priorities in every interaction.

No Judgement

I hold space for people to explore their experiences with curiosity and discovery. Every journey is unique, and I honor yours without judgement.

Empower

I build on existing strengths and provide resources and tools to facilitate growth and learning. You already have what you need - I help you to use it

Support

I work with my clients in partnership to build trust in relationship. Your goals become our shared mission, and I walk alongside you every step of the way.
